Who we are

UKTaxCal (uktaxcal.com) is run by AlwaysOnPlanB. You can contact us through alwaysonplanb.com/contact.html.

Your calculator figures stay on your device

The numbers you type into our calculators are worked out in your browser. They are not sent to us and we do not store them.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used so we can improve it. It records things like the pages you visit, how long you stay, how you arrived (for example from a search engine), your device and browser type, and your approximate location such as your country or city.

Google processes this data for us as our processor under the Google Ads Data Processing Terms. We have turned off Google signals and sharing data with Google for its own products and services, and we do not use Google Analytics for advertising. Google Analytics keeps this data for 14 months, then deletes it.

We rely on the statistical purposes exception in the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (as updated by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025) and on our legitimate interest in improving the site under UK GDPR. You can turn analytics off at any time below.
