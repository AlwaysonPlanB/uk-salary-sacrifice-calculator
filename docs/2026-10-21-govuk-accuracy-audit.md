# UKTaxCal accuracy audit against GOV.UK, 2026/27 (checked 21 October 2026)

Scope: every file in js/, profiles/ (7), blog/ (5), index.html, llms.txt read in full. Figures checked against the GOV.UK content API (pages fetched 21 Oct 2026). Calculations reproduced by running the site's own js/tax-engine.js, js/salary-sacrifice.js and js/warnings.js in Node with the inputs shown. Nothing in the repo was changed.

## A. Summary

- WRONG: 29 (1 changes a calculated result: the BIK table, L9; 28 are wrong stated facts, wording or links)
- LOGIC: 17 (10 High, 4 Medium, 3 Low; the figure is right but applied wrongly, or the code is wrong)
- OUTDATED: 5
- UNVERIFIED: 9
- OK: 46 items verified (section C)

The biggest problems, all High:
1. Income tax engine taxes at 45% (Scotland 48%) from £112,570 of taxable income, not £125,140. Anyone earning over about £116,760 is overcharged by up to £628.50.
2. Class 2 NI (£189.80) is charged to every sole trader with profits of £12,570 or more. GOV.UK says none is payable at profits of £7,105 or more.
3. Dividend tax engine: unused Personal Allowance is never set against dividends; the basic band grows when the PA tapers; and the £500 allowance does not use up band. Directors and investors get wrong results, and the £0 and £5,000 salary rows in the "optimal salary" tables are overstated.
4. Director page passes the salary in as the BIK tax rate. A £40,000 EV gives £20,112,000 of BIK tax.
5. The company car BIK percentage tables do not match GOV.UK for 2026/27.
6. Pension annual allowance taper starts at £200,000 of ANI instead of adjusted income over £260,000 (with threshold income over £200,000).
7. Section 24 credit is not capped at property profits. Payments on account include Class 2 and student loan.
8. The Scottish taper zone marginal rate is 63%; it should be 67.5%.
